## 🧠 5. Reflection Questions

### 5.1 What would happen if two PCs had the same IP address?
_ANSWER = IF two PCs have the same IP address, it causes an ip conflict. Both computer may have network connection problems and cannot communicate properly.

### 5.2 Why do we use a switch instead of directly connecting PCs together?
_ANSWER=We use a switch to connect multiple PCs in one network. it allows computers  to communicate and share information easily.

### 5.3 What does the subnet mask 255.255.255.0 indicate?
ANSWER= The subnet mask 255.255.255.0 means the first three parts of the IP Address identify the network and the last part identifies the device.
